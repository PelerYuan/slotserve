### Added

- **QuickBook**: Overview, Schedule "New booking" and Dev environments "Book an environment" open a short dialog that asks what, when (Now · 1 h / 2 h / until 18:00, or a time) and picks a free GPU automatically ("Change" lists every lane with the reason it can or cannot be used). A refusal says why (quota with the hours left, who has the GPU) and offers the next free time or the waitlist. The full booking dialog stays under "More options" (repeat weekly, note, presets, booking for others) and for dragging across the calendar. The choice comes from the new `POST /api/placement/fit`, which applies the same rules as booking itself.
