### Changed

- `docker-compose.yml` pins the image to a minor version (`ghcr.io/peleryuan/slotserve:0.10`, which gets 0.10.x fixes) instead of `latest`, and limits the container's Docker log to 5 files of 50 MB.
- Release candidates (`vX.Y.Z-rc1` tags) are published as `X.Y.Z-rc1` and `rc` only; `latest` and `X.Y` move only with a final release.
